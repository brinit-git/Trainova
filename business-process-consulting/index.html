Your people aren’t slow. The work is waiting.

Walk one order through your business with a stopwatch. Most of its life, nobody is working on it. It’s sitting in a tray, in a chat, on someone’s desk, or waiting for you to come back from a meeting.

So you hire another person, and it gets a little worse, because now there’s one more person to hand it to. Payroll goes up. Speed doesn’t.

Business process consulting starts by making that wait visible. Most owners are surprised less by how bad it is than by how small the fix turns out to be. Our business process consulting services focus on process improvement you can see in a month, not a slide deck.

Our process

How we fix a process.

  1. Follow the work

    We pick a core flow such as enquiry to invoice and follow real jobs through it.

    You get: a process map, warts included

  2. Find the waits

    Where does work sit? Whose signature does it need? Where’s the rework coming from?

    You get: the three blockages costing most

  3. Redesign with the team

    The people doing the work remove steps with us. They usually know which ones are pointless.

    You get: a simpler flow

  4. Run it on real jobs

    We test on live work, adjust what breaks, and only then make it the standard.

    You get: a tested process and SOP

  5. Automate what’s left

    Once the flow is simple, we look at where a tool can help. Often it’s smaller than you’d expect.

    You get: a list of what to automate

  6. Track it

    Before and after numbers, reviewed regularly, so improvements don’t quietly slide back.

    You get: a few numbers you can trust

Questions about business process consulting

What does a business process consultant do?

A business process consultant maps how work moves through your company, finds where it gets stuck, gets repeated or falls through the cracks, and redesigns the flow so it needs fewer people, fewer steps and fewer decisions from the top.

How is process consulting different from SOP consulting?

Process consulting decides what the right way of working should be. SOP consulting writes it down so people can follow it. If you document a broken process, you just get a very clear broken process, so we usually look at the flow first.

What does process optimisation mean in a small business?

Usually nothing fancy: removing an approval nobody needs, combining two registers into one, making one person responsible for a handover. Most of the gains in an MSME come from clearing simple blockages, not from new technology.

How do you measure improvement?

We pick a few numbers before we start, such as how long an order takes from enquiry to delivery, how often work is redone, or how many decisions reach the owner each week. Then we track those same numbers afterwards.

Will this disrupt my business while you work?

We change one process at a time and leave the rest alone. Your team keeps working while the improved version is tested on real jobs.
