Why business coaching wears off by Wednesday.

Good coaching changes how you see things, and that’s real. Plenty of Kerala business owners have been helped by it, and we won’t tell you otherwise.

But think about what happens next. You come back seeing clearly, into a team that wasn’t in the room. Nothing around you has changed. You announce some new ideas, your staff wait for it to pass, and it passes. You start to feel the failure was yours.

It wasn’t. A business doesn’t run on what the owner knows. It runs on what’s written down, trained and checked. That part can’t be done from a stage.

The real comparison

Coach or consultant? Ask what’s left after the last session.

  • What you get from most coaching

    A sharper view, more discipline and a group of people who understand you. Worth having, especially if you feel alone at the top.

  • What you get from us

    SOPs, role definitions, delegation limits, KRAs and trained managers. Things your team uses on a Tuesday when you’re not there.

  • Who it’s aimed at

    A coach works mainly with you. We work with you and the people you need to stop calling you.

  • Where it happens

    A hotel ballroom or a video call, usually. For us, your office, your real work and your real numbers.

Questions about business coaching

Who is the best business coach in Kerala?

Kerala has several well-known business coaches, and “No.1” claims are self-awarded, so we’d ignore them, ours included. Whether you’re comparing the top business coach in Kochi or someone in Trivandrum, ask each one what you’ll physically have after the programme, who will work with your team, and whether you can speak to two past clients of your size.

What is the difference between a business coach and a business consultant?

A coach usually works on you: your thinking, goals, confidence and discipline. A consultant usually works on the business: its processes, structure and systems. Many owners benefit from both. We’re mostly the second, with leadership development for you and your managers.

What are business coach fees in India?

Guides on the Indian market quote roughly ₹5,000 to ₹25,000 per session and ₹20,000 to ₹2 lakh a month for one-to-one coaching. Group programmes vary widely. Our plans are ₹9,999 to ₹49,999 a month, and they include work on your business, not only with you.

Are you a business trainer in Kerala who runs seminars?

Our main work is inside your business, with your team. We also run training programmes and working sessions, but we’re not a seminar company, and that’s a deliberate choice.

Can I find a business mentor in Kerala who works in Malayalam?

Yes. We work in Malayalam and English, with whichever is more comfortable for you and your team.

I’ve already done a coaching programme. Is this still useful?

Very often. You may already know what needs to change. What’s usually missing is the months of unglamorous work with your team to make it real. That’s the part we do.
