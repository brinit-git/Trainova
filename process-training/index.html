Six months after the SOP project

“We made the SOPs. Nobody opens the file.”

It cost real money. It’s on the shared drive. Everyone’s doing what they did before.

Process training that gets your team to actually follow the SOP.

A written process only matters once people do it without thinking. We train your team on your processes until they do, and show your managers how to keep it going.

People don’t resist SOPs. They resist being told.

Picture someone who’s done the job for nine years. Then a document appears that says they’ve been doing it wrong, written by someone who’s never done it. Of course they ignore it. You would too.

Process training isn’t reading the document aloud in a hall. It’s showing why a step exists, what goes wrong without it, letting people practise on real work and listening when they spot something that won’t work. Sometimes they’re right, and the SOP should change.

After that, it’s habit, and habits come from someone checking. That’s the part nobody plans for.

How a rollout works

From document to habit.

  1. Review the SOP

    Is it clear, short and true? We fix it first if not.

  2. Explain the why

    What goes wrong without this step. People remember reasons, not rules.

  3. Practise on real work

    Doing it, not hearing about it, with mistakes corrected on the spot.

  4. Equip the manager

    A simple way to check, and how to correct without humiliating.

  5. Audit it

    After a few weeks, we look at what’s followed and what’s being skipped.

  6. Fix or change

    If everyone skips a step, maybe the step is wrong.

Questions about process training

What is process training?

Process training teaches employees how a job should be done in your company, step by step, and why. It’s how a written SOP turns into something people do without looking it up.

How do you get staff to follow SOPs?

Involve them in writing it, explain why it matters, practise on real work, have their manager check it, and audit it regularly. Most SOPs fail at this stage, not in the writing.

Can you train staff on SOPs someone else wrote?

Yes. We’ll review them first. If they’re unclear or don’t match how the work is done, we’ll tell you, because training people on a broken document makes things worse.

What is “Scaling Businesses with Systems and SOPs”?

It’s our signature programme for owners and senior teams: how to think in systems, what to document first and how to roll it out. It’s a good starting point if you want to build the capability inside your own company.

How long does process training take?

A single process can take an hour. A rollout across a business runs over weeks, one group of processes at a time, so nothing gets forgotten and work keeps moving.
